## Intro

🤩 HI! This is my first _drop_! It's been a while since I've wanted to make a newsletter for graficos.net. So, I present to you _*GRAFICOZZ*_. I want to share with you some of the latest news on the Web platform and some cool libraries I encounter while unceasingly browsing the web.

I'm a big fan of other newsletters, human-made zines, and everything that's people-focused and an alternative to the "corporate web." Newsletters have helped me stay up to date with the latest news in Web development throughout my career, but lately, I've found in them a connection with other humans in a way that AI-generated content can't evoke. This is also my way of staying close to the indie-web-DIY spirit—building this underground-style newsletter. (Yes, I use em dashes.)

## The links

- 🤖 [shadcn-ui/lint](https://github.com/shadcn-ui/lint) — _An agent-first linter for Tailwind design systems. Write design system rules that agents can verify._ Something very interesting if you're into Design Systems. I feel like the more we let agents touch our code, the more important guardrails are: for conventions, architecture, and of course, design systems as a core piece in the middle of medium to large companies.
- 🤯 [This post by Agustin Capeletto](https://noscript.ing/compute) will blow your mind. He used the browser's native `layout` to build a physics engine in pure CSS. Pure genius.
- 🧑‍💻 [Unframework](https://unframework.dev/), by Alex Grozav — a fresh take to a "unified language for UI components" that compile to all major UI frameworks. Inspired by TSX and Vue.
- 💌 [Maizzle](https://maizzle.com/) — A newsletter engine for developers I didn't know! Based in Vue's and Nuxt common patterns (templates, composables) and Tailwind. And, guess what? It's what is powering this newsletter and part of what inspired me to start the project. Thank you, Maizzle!
