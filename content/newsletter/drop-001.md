## Intro

Hi! This is my first _drop_! It's been a while since I wanted to make a Newsletter for graficos.net. So I present you _*GRAFICOZZ*_. I will use to to share cool things I encounter on the web. The latest news in the platform and some cool libraries.

I am a big fan of other newsletters and they have helped me stay up-to-date with the latest news in Web Development. This is also my way of staying DIY with this underground-style newsletter.

## The links

- [shadcn-ui/lint](https://github.com/shadcn-ui/lint) — _An agent-first linter for Tailwind design systems. Write design system rules that agents can verify._ Something very interesting if you're into Design Systems.
- [This post by Agustin Capeletto](https://noscript.ing/compute) will blow your mind. He used browser's native layout to build a physics engine in pure CSS.
- [Unframework](https://unframework.dev/), by Alex Grozav — a fresh take to a unified language for UI components that compile to all major UI frameworks. Inspired by TSX and Vue.
- [Maizzle](https://maizzle.com/) — A newsletter engine for developers I didn't know! Based in Vue's and Nuxt common patterns (templates, composables) and Tailwind. It's what is powering this first _drop_.
